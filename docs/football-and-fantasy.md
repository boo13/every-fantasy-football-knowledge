# Football and fantasy in one sitting

Reviewed 2026-08-31. This is a general introduction, not the rules of any particular league.

## The actual sport

The offense advances the ball by running or passing. It generally has four attempts, called downs, to gain ten yards and earn another set. A touchdown is worth six real-game points. Field goals score three; the attempt after a touchdown can add one or two. Real-game points and fantasy points are different systems. [NFL terms](https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/nfl-football-basics/football-terms)

## Your fantasy team

You draft players from different NFL teams. Their individual statistics earn fantasy points; your starters' scores are added together. A real team can lose while one of its players produces an excellent fantasy score. Bench players usually contribute no points to your weekly matchup. In most redraft formats you build a new roster each season; dynasty and keeper leagues retain some or all players. [Sleeper beginner guide](https://sleeper.com/blog/what-is-fantasy-football/)

| Position | What they do | Fantasy relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QB, quarterback | Directs the offense and throws; sometimes runs | Passing volume, touchdowns, rushing |
| RB, running back | Runs and sometimes catches passes | Carries, catches, goal-line role |
| WR, wide receiver | Runs routes to receive passes | Targets, catches, receiving yards |
| TE, tight end | Mixes blocking and receiving | Receiving role varies greatly |
| K, kicker | Attempts field goals and extra points | Rules often vary by kick distance |
| D/ST, defense/special teams | An NFL unit, not one offensive player | Sacks, takeaways, scores, points allowed |
| FLEX | A roster slot accepting multiple positions | Check eligible positions; superflex may include QB |

Position background: [NFL terms](https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/nfl-football-basics/football-terms). Fantasy roster eligibility is platform/league-specific.

## Scoring changes player value

PPR awards a point per catch; half-PPR awards half a point; non-PPR awards none for the catch itself. Yardage and touchdown scoring still apply. Some leagues add position-specific bonuses or change passing-touchdown values. [Reception bonuses](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/3652730-how-are-reception-bonuses-calculated), [custom scoring](https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/3998131-what-scoring-options-are-available)

Illustration, **not league defaults**: under 0.1 points per receiving yard, six catches for 60 yards and no touchdown earn 6 non-PPR points, 9 half-PPR, or 12 PPR. That arithmetic is why a generic player ranking is not enough.

## The recurring work

Draft a roster; check who plays and when; set starters before their lock times; use waivers/free agency to replace or improve players; cover bye weeks; evaluate trades. Deadlines, waiver order, budgets, injured-reserve eligibility, and playoffs are league rules, not universal facts. [NFL fantasy FAQ](https://www.nfl.com/news/faq-for-fantasy-football-beginners-09000d5d808cbbec)
