This is a **selective, unranked watchlist of 12 offensive rookies**. It combines verified draft identity with the strongest relevant team reporting located in this pass. It is not a comprehensive rookie class, a current injury clearance, an ADP table, or a projection. The order groups positions and teams, not expected fantasy value. No league settings, private rosters, or participant information are assumed.

## How to read the evidence

For a rookie, several different questions must remain separate: Was the player drafted? Did the player make a roster? Is the player available for the next game? What offensive role has actually been earned? How valuable is that role under a particular scoring system? Answering the first two does not answer the rest.

**Facts** below are narrow statements supported by original team announcements or reporting. **Interpretation** describes conditional fantasy relevance, not a forecast. **Unknowns** and **next evidence** identify what a later researcher should check. Where a team reporter predicts a workload, that prediction stays attributed to the reporter instead of becoming a coaching commitment.

## Quarterback

### Fernando Mendoza — Las Vegas Raiders

**Verified facts.** Las Vegas selected the Indiana quarterback first overall. The August 30 roster analysis retained Mendoza, Kirk Cousins, and Aidan O'Connell. That article establishes the three-player quarterback room, not a new starting-quarterback announcement. [Raiders draft announcement, April 23](https://www.raiders.com/news/fernando-mendoza-no-1-overall-pick-raiders-quarterback-2026-nfl-draft-indiana-football); [initial-roster observations, August 30](https://www.raiders.com/news/raiders-initial-2026-53-man-roster-observations-quarterbacks-2026-draft-class).

The team's July 29 camp report described Cousins beginning camp with the first unit and Mendoza working with the second unit after offseason work with the third. This is a **dated camp arrangement**, not proof of the September depth chart. [Raiders camp report, July 29; Spanish-language original](https://www.raiders.com/news/arranca-el-campo-de-entrenamiento-2026-con-fernando-mendoza-continuando-su-progreso).

**Interpretation.** His investment and developmental path make him a relevant long-horizon research subject. Near-term fantasy usefulness depends first on whether he will actually play. Two-quarterback or superflex formats can change the value of that contingency; a one-quarterback format need not value it the same way.

**Unknowns.** This pass has not established a Week 1 starting announcement, a planned handover date, or recurring rushing/package usage. Preseason improvement cannot fill those gaps.

**Next evidence.** Check the coach's explicit starting decision, game-status reports and active list, then actual first-team starts and designed quarterback runs. If he remains a reserve, preserve that outcome rather than treating draft position as a failed statistical projection that was never made.

## Weekly research instructions

Use this guide to choose small, answerable questions rather than repeatedly scraping the entire rookie class.

1. **Before a decision:** recheck official availability and transactions for the relevant players. Openers do not all occur on the same weekday; verify the actual schedule and report timing.
2. **After games:** record the specific game/week and whether the source covers the complete game. Keep team offensive volume, individual opportunity, efficiency and touchdowns separate. This repository does not automatically collect routes, snaps or red-zone usage.
3. **For comparisons over time:** use the same definition and denominator where possible. Compare similar availability/personnel situations; explicitly mark injury replacements, limited workloads and unusual game scripts.
4. **For a changed interpretation:** add a dated research briefing linking this baseline and the new evidence. State which uncertainty resolved, which remains, and whether an earlier expectation was actually recorded. Do not rewrite this cutoff as though later results were already known.
5. **For a genuine forecast:** create a separate, pre-outcome hypothesis with a measurable outcome, source coverage and invalidation conditions. This watchlist itself contains no ranked forecast and earns no predictive-accuracy credit merely because a player later succeeds.

## Coverage and source limitations

- These 12 players were selected for a mix of substantial draft investment, sourced current role/availability questions and one later-round roster case. Selection is editorial and incomplete; omission is **not** a negative player evaluation. Other drafted and undrafted rookies may be relevant.
- Veteran newcomers, kickers, defenders and complete offensive-line changes are outside this pass. Follow the [team reference](team-reference.md) to extend research with original team sources.
- The guide verifies draft/team identity but creates no cross-provider identity mappings. Missing GSIS IDs and absent rookie NFL statistics remain missing; college totals do not become NFL totals.
- Team sites are original sources for their own transactions and reports, but their analysis and projections are still interpretation. A roster gallery, press-release list and a report from the same club are not three independent confirmations.
- Source prose can contain mistakes. The August 30 Jets page says the active roster was at 52 while its gallery is labeled 53; this guide uses the named inclusions rather than claiming a reconciled roster total. The Seattle August 8 report has inconsistent weekday wording; the later dated participation report is used without relying on that weekday.
- No current ADP, projected fantasy points, proprietary route tables, full articles or personal information were copied. For draft cost and format assumptions, use the separately dated [public ADP/projection audit](public-adp-and-projections.md).

Ask for any league rules privately; do not save them here.
