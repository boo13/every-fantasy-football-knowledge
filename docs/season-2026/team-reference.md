## What each team page is useful for

Use the official site's news and transactions pages to verify signings, releases, reserve-list moves, and announced quarterback changes. Read its actual practice/game-status report when available. A marketing profile, a preseason lineup graphic, or an old article is not an official game-day availability decision.

The directory is complete; current player research is deliberately selective. The absence of a team from the briefing means **not investigated in this pass**, not healthy, stable, or unimportant. Avoid generating 32 speculative depth charts merely to fill a table.

## Calendar traps worth checking

Week 1 spans **September 9–10 and 13–14**, rather than starting with the usual Thursday/Sunday pattern. The Rams–49ers game is in Melbourne. Source: [NFL Football Operations calendar](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates), checked September 1 UTC.

Do not infer local kickoff time from a city's name. Check the official schedule, the timezone in the source, and the fantasy platform's actual lock rules. Bye weeks and flexed games belong in dated schedule evidence, not in an undated memorized team profile. The [current collection](../../context/CURRENT.md) is a starting point, not live schedule monitoring.
