## What not to conclude

Do not call a player healthy because an injury field is blank. Do not equate trending adds with the best waiver choice. Do not use last year's team label as the player's current team. Do not infer that a player with no NFL stats is worthless: rookies and changing roles need other evidence. These are evidence-handling cautions, not empirical player predictions.

The collected ESPN entries are discovery links only; this briefing makes no claim that the underlying stories were independently verified.
