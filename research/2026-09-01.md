## Decision implications

- A rookie's NFL selection and roster membership are evidence of investment and current roster context, not a forecast of touches or starts. Compare public role reports with actual game usage when available.
- Availability and workload need separate updates. A reserve transaction, practice return and official active status answer different questions.
- Scoring, lineup slots and horizon determine whether a contingent or developmental role matters. Obtain those settings privately; this note does not prescribe draft selections or bids.
- Current-season statistics remain absent in the referenced collection. Do not substitute the prior season, college totals, preseason highlights or an empty-row zero.

## Coverage and next live checks

The library now has broader explanatory coverage, but it still lacks live official inactives, complete snaps/routes/red-zone data, automatic projections/ADP, current weather and private player availability. K/DEF catalog presence does not provide kicking or defensive production; IDP is outside the offensive dataset.

Use each game's actual reporting schedule, not a fixed Friday rule. The [NFL's 2026 calendar](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates) sets different reporting days for different kickoff days. Before any real decision, recheck official transactions, participation and game status, then verify available alternatives and platform rules privately. A green weekly data run does not certify completed news research or present-day clearance.
