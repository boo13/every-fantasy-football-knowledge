## Calendar: why roster news dominates this week

The NFL calendar places final roster reduction on August 30, waiver-claim processing/practice-squad formation on August 31, and kickoff across September 9–10 and 13–14. Week 1 injury-report timing differs for Wednesday, Thursday, Sunday, and Monday games. A generic “check Friday” routine is insufficient for every player this week. [NFL Football Operations calendar](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates), observed September 1 UTC.

**Interpretation:** cutdown-week aggregate adds can reflect a player merely securing an NFL roster place. That is different from earning a high-volume fantasy role. A reserve-list designation or released teammate can change the opportunity set without establishing how many touches remain.

**Verified fact:** an August 31 Cowboys report identifies Davis as the backup behind Javonte Williams, confirms Blue was waived, and records Emari Demercado's waiver arrival. [Cowboys: RB decisions, August 31 at 3:24 p.m. as displayed](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/schottenheimer-explains-jaydon-blue-rb-decisions-for-cowboys-roster).
